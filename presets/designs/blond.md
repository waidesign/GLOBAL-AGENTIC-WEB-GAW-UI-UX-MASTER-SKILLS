# BLOND — Style Reference
> product plinth, citron signature. Let a tight mixed-scale product gallery carry the page, with small medium-weight labels and a full-screen yellow-green closing field.

**Theme:** light

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

BLOND is an industrial-design portfolio with almost no typographic hierarchy between projects: small Monument Grotesk labels let large product photographs do the work. Gallery White #f9f9f9 and Studio Ink #1d1717 form the quiet base, with 10px media corners and narrow gutters connecting full-width and paired project tiles. Navigation stays small and transparent, while a compact Selected and All switch sits centrally. The page changes register at its Citron Field #e9fe79 footer, where 30px contact details and an oversized vector wordmark replace the gallery. After choosing Work, the site is precise and media-led rather than an oversized headline landing page.

## Typography

### Monument Grotesk — The sole measured text face, exposed by CSS as monumentGrotesk and loaded at weight 500. Almost all gallery and navigation text is 14px with 16.8px leading and -0.14px tracking; the footer contacts rise to 30px with 31.5px leading and -0.6px tracking. The huge BLOND wordmark is a vector, not another measured display role.

## Components

### Transparent Navigation Bar
**Role:** Work and studio navigation

Keep the measured 47px transparent header with Work at the left 10px edge and Office at the right. Use Monument Grotesk 14px/500 at 16.8px leading and -0.14px tracking. Active text is Studio Ink #1d1717; inactive text uses the measured 50-percent ink appearance.

### Compact Gallery Switch
**Role:** Selected and all-project views

Center the Selected and All switch above the gallery in a compact 8px-corner track. Use Monument Grotesk 14px/500 labels and small counts, 5px internal spacing and the measured pale translucent surface. Keep it horizontal and quiet rather than making it a large pill CTA.

### Wide Project Card
**Role:** Full-width featured product

Place the featured project across the 12-column grid with 10px media corners and no shadow. The measured opening card is about 1580px by 889px at a 1600px viewport, beginning around 145px from the top. Let the photograph fill the card rather than adding an editorial headline alongside it.

### Paired Project Card
**Role:** Alternating product grid

Use two 6-column project tiles between wider works, preserving the observed portrait and landscape ratios. Maintain the 10px gallery gutters and 10px base corners. Keep each project’s actual photographic background instead of normalizing all assets into the same neutral thumbnail.

### Overlay Project Label
**Role:** Project title and metadata

Place a small Gallery White #f9f9f9 label over the product image, using Studio Ink #1d1717 Monument Grotesk 14px/500 text at 16.8px leading and -0.14px tracking. Keep the title compact with a right arrow; secondary discipline and year text uses Quiet Graphite #8b8888.

### Contact Copy Button
**Role:** Copy studio email addresses

Use transparent square text controls on Citron Field #e9fe79, with Studio Ink #1d1717 Monument Grotesk 30px/500 text at 31.5px leading and -0.6px tracking. Preserve the approximately 32px measured line-height box and adjacent copy feedback instead of adding a filled button.

### Citron Studio Footer

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Gallery White #f9f9f9 with Studio Ink #1d1717 for the quiet product gallery.
- Set ordinary labels and navigation in Monument Grotesk 14px/500 at 16.8px leading and -0.14px tracking.
- Keep 10px media corners and 10px gallery gutters, alternating wide 12-column and paired 6-column projects.
- Reserve Citron Field #e9fe79 for the large closing studio field and introductory identity treatment.
- Set footer contacts at 30px/500 with 31.5px leading and -0.6px tracking.
- Keep project captions small and over the image, allowing the photography to supply the hierarchy.
- Use the actual Work control to leave the introductory film before measuring or recording the portfolio.

### Don't
- Do not replace the gallery with a giant text hero; preserve the small-label product-led opening.
- Do not spread Citron Field #e9fe79 across every card or use it as a universal CTA fill.
- Do not force the product images into identical small cards; retain varied ratios and wide interruptions.
- Do not add shadows or structural outlines to the flat Gallery White #f9f9f9 canvas.
- Do not increase ordinary labels beyond the measured 14px role or change their weight from 500 by default.
- Do not treat the enormous vector wordmark as a measured 300px text font role.
- Do not record a locked intro while programmatically scrolling the gallery underneath it; enter Work first.

## Elevation

No box shadows or structural borders were measured. Photographic products supply the depth; the actual interface is a flat Gallery White field, tight gutters and compact overlaid labels. Project media use the observed 10px rounding, while the filter uses an 8px corner. The footer is one unbroken Citron Field, with its large vector identity anchored to the bottom.

## Surfaces

- **Citron Field** (`#e9fe79`) — Full-screen studio footer and introductory brand treatment; the accent is concentrated rather than spread across project cards.
- **Gallery White** (`#f9f9f9`) — Continuous gallery canvas and small light labels floating over the product imagery.

## Imagery

Use large, deliberately art-directed product photographs and films of actual industrial objects: medical pens, appliances, packaging, lighting, consumer hardware and furniture. Backgrounds vary with each project, from very pale studio stills to nearly black fields; do not force them into one color grade. Give media 10px corners and let full-width images interrupt paired tiles. Small light labels sit on top of the imagery and identify brand, project, discipline and year. The introductory studio film can be skipped through Work; the library recording captures the actual project gallery. The footer wordmark is a vector brand asset, not display text to recreate with a guessed font.

## Layout

Use a full-width 12-column portfolio grid with 10px outer placement and narrow 10px gutters. Open the project grid below about 145px of top space, leaving the fixed transparent navigation and central filter above it. Alternate 12-column landscape projects with paired 6-column portrait or landscape works; avoid equal-height template cards. A measured full-width project is about 1580px wide by 889px high at this 1600px viewport. Keep captions as small over-image controls rather than separate large text blocks. After the long selected-project sequence, end in a 1000px-high Citron Field footer with a left studio statement, 30px contact stack toward the right, small social/legal links and a full-width wordmark at its lower edge.
