# Stereoscope Coffee — Style Reference
> paper bag, ruled atlas. Build square, hairline-divided commerce around quiet grotesque labels, animated packaging, and generous monochrome editorial panels.

**Theme:** light

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Stereoscope Coffee is an architectural coffee storefront where fine black rules connect product, editorial and store information into one continuous grid. Paper #ffffff alternates with Gallery Mist #f3f3f3, Packaging Gray #d9d9d9 and Carbon Panel #1d1d1b. Brunswick Grotesque stays predominantly at weight 400, with 16px uppercase navigation and a restrained 30px display scale. Rotating packaging, film and photography supply the movement and color; square controls, 30px panel padding, a border-built footer and an enormous vector wordmark keep the interface itself precise and flat.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Paper #ffffff, Gallery Mist #f3f3f3 and Packaging Gray #d9d9d9 as broad fields with Ink #000000 rules.
- Set the dominant interface in Brunswick Grotesque 16px/400 at 24px leading and 0px tracking.
- Keep manifesto type at 30px/400 with 36px leading, and uppercase collection headings at 24px/400 with 32px leading.
- Apply 0px corners and no shadows to interface panels, product media, forms and buttons.
- Use 30px panel padding, 1px grid gaps and 1px black dividers to build the connected information grid.
- Pair animated packaging with actual photography and film, preserving equal-width editorial halves and varied product scale.
- Reserve Carbon Panel #1d1d1b for dark media and square Subscribe controls; keep Status Red #fb2c36 confined to tiny store indicators.

### Don't
- Do not round ordinary controls or product cards; their measured interface radius is 0px.
- Do not add card shadows; the page uses 1px rules and contrasting flat fields for separation.
- Do not replace Brunswick Grotesque 400 with bold display headings or add negative tracking to the 16px labels.
- Do not treat the giant vector wordmark as a separate measured text font; adapt its role using the new brand identity.
- Do not promote Status Red #fb2c36 to the primary CTA fill; Subscribe uses Carbon Panel #1d1d1b.
- Do not standardize all product images into identical small tiles; preserve the large-feature versus smaller-package hierarchy.
- Do not substitute glossy gradients for Packaging Gray #d9d9d9 and Gallery Mist #f3f3f3 media grounds.
