# Neutra VDL — Style Reference
> surveyor's ledger, glass house. Rule warm bone pages into four strict columns of medium-weight grotesque, then let drawings and photographs answer a numbered index.

**Theme:** mixed

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Neutra VDL is a house-museum site organised like an architect's drawing set. Drafting Bone #f7f4ef pages carry Redwood Ink #120902 type split across a four-column masthead above a single 1px rule, and every route is numbered, from 1 to 6.3, in a dark Contents Charcoal #1e1e1e index that slides over the page. Akzidenz-Grotesk Pro at weight 500 and tight negative tracking does all the talking: there is no second family, no accent colour and no corner radius. Colour arrives only through photography of the house, while the signature block is an exploded axonometric drawing whose six numbered rows light up one level at a time.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Akzidenz-Grotesk Pro alone, with 500 for headings and navigation and 400 for rows and captions.
- Set the masthead at 74.44px/500 with -3.72px tracking, with one word opening each of four columns above a 1px Redwood Ink #120902 rule.
- Keep a 51.11px page margin and four 357.8px columns with 22.22px gutters on every route.
- Use 1px rules in #120902 on bone and #f7f4ef on charcoal to separate rows, in place of cards or borders.
- Apply a Drafting Bone #f7f4ef hover band across the row in the dark index, turning its text to Contents Charcoal #1e1e1e.
- Reserve Contents Charcoal #1e1e1e for the index and for archival reading routes such as Media and History.
- Pair every destination with its decimal number, such as 2.1 or 4.2, at the row's right edge.

### Don't
- Do not introduce a chromatic accent; colour comes only from photographs of the house.
- Do not round images, buttons or rows; every corner is 0px except the 50px circular map marker.
- Do not add box-shadows or floating cards; separate layers with a 1px rule or a full inversion.
- Do not centre the masthead or set it in one run; spread its words across the four-column grid.
- Do not use pure black #000000 for headings and rules; those are Redwood Ink #120902.
- Do not set headings at weight 700; bold is limited to the 22.22px appendix bars.
- Do not open with full-bleed imagery; the first screen is type and rules, and photography waits below it.

## Elevation

Depth does not exist here: no measured box-shadow on any of the ten states, and no radius except the round map markers. Layers separate by inversion instead, as the Contents Charcoal #1e1e1e index wipes over Drafting Bone #f7f4ef, and by 1px rules in Redwood Ink or reversed bone that do the structural work other sites give to cards.

## Imagery

Three registers share the page, all square-cornered and flat. Full-colour architectural photography shows the house itself, with brushed aluminium, rust-orange doors, reflected Silver Lake and planting; it supplies the only colour on the site. Black-and-white archival prints and scanned drawings fill the Archive and the Media grid, cropped to near-square 357.8px tiles and fading in from a #e3ddd5 or #262626 placeholder. The signature image is a thin-line exploded axonometric of the house on bare bone, where the selected level stays dark while the others fade back. There are no icons beyond arrow glyphs on outbound rows and the two-line menu toggle, and no illustration or texture. The Archive adds a small thumbnail that follows the cursor across a large print.

## Layout

Every route sits on the same four-column grid: 51.11px margins, 357.8px columns and 22.22px gutters, with no maximum width because the root font scales with the viewport. The homepage opens with a 284px empty band above the masthead, one word per column on a 1px rule, then details at left, three paragraphs in the third column and text links in the fourth. A photograph fills the last three columns, then a double-column history, then a full-screen axonometric with the six-row selector at left and a footer in small print. Inner pages follow the pattern with a count at left and a title in the third column: Media runs a four-up numbered grid, Program a ruled event list. The root is locked and sections translate under a wheel-driven smoother, so the top bar hides during scrolling. The Contents index covers the whole viewport, with a section column, a page column and numbers flush right.
