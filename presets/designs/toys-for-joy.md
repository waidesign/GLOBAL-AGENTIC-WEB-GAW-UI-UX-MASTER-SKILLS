# Toys for Joy — Style Reference
> crumpled plum poster, lavender love letter. Set towering hairline-condensed capitals in dusky plum over soft lavender fields and let one arched photograph break the type.

**Theme:** light

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Toys for Joy is a lavender-washed sexual-wellness storefront that behaves like an art-nouveau poster rather than a shop grid. A crumpled purple paper hero carries Lilac Haze #d7c6ee capitals, then the page settles onto Lavender Paper #f5ecff and Seashell Paper #fdf4ee with Dahlia Plum #5d4b7a type, broken by full-bleed colour fields: a split plum and Mauve Panel #817895 product block, a Sky Wash #e6f1ff discovery section and a Wisteria Band #897aa9 sign-up. Dahlia condensed at 400 does all the talking at 112–304px with sub-1.0 line-heights, while Aeonik Light 300 whispers in 16px body and 12.8px uppercase labels. No shadows, almost no radius: depth comes from arch masks, stepped bands and colour shifts.

## Typography

### Dahlia condensed — The font files name this face Dahlia (Condensed cut), served as "Dahlia condensed". The whole personality of the page. A high-contrast, hairline-flared condensed display face set only at weight 400, from 48px feature titles to 304px word-wall capitals, always with zero tracking and line-heights collapsed to 0.68–1.0 so stacked lines nearly touch. Uppercase for posters, mixed case for softer lines.
- **Substitute:** Italiana
- **Licence:** commercial (the font file names it Dahlia); buy a licence from its foundry, or use the substitute
- **Weights:** 400
- **Sizes:** 48px, 112px, 128px, 137.6px, 160px, 192px, 224px, 304px
- **Line height:** 0.68, 0.76, 0.80, 0.85, 0.90, 1.00
- **Letter spacing:** 0px at every size from 48px to 304px

### Aeonik — A neo-grotesk used almost entirely at Light 300: 16px body at 20.8px line-height, 12.8px uppercase labels at 1.1, 9.6px and 11.2px legal lines with +0.2px tracking. Its thin, quiet strokes leave all the drama to Dahlia; weight 400 appears only on a few 12.8px footer product links.
- **Substitute:** DM Sans
- **Licence:** commercial; buy a licence from its foundry, or use the substitute
- **Weights:** 300, 400
- **Sizes:** 9.6px, 11.2px, 12.8px, 14.4px, 16px
- **Line height:** 1.00, 1.10, 1.20, 1.25, 1.30
- **Letter spacing:** +0.48px at 12.8px tilted labels; +0.2px at 9.6px and 11.2px; 0px at 16px

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Lavender Paper #f5ecff as the default canvas and Seashell Paper #fdf4ee for the warm intro bands under the hero.
- Set every headline in Dahlia condensed 400 with 0px tracking and line-heights between 0.68 and 1.0, so stacked lines nearly touch.
- Set Dahlia Plum #5d4b7a display type on light fields and Lilac Haze #d7c6ee or Lavender Paper #f5ecff type on purple fields.
- Keep body copy in Aeonik 16px/300 at 20.8px line-height and labels in 12.8px/300 uppercase at 1.1 line-height.
- Apply full-bleed colour fields as section breaks: a split 800px Dahlia Plum and Mauve Panel #817895 block, a Sky Wash #e6f1ff field and a Wisteria Band #897aa9 band.
- Use 9999px radius only for the four-dot menu toggle, and 7px only for the footer card.
- Reserve 160px between major sections and 16px between elements, on the 4px base unit.

### Don't
- Do not use bold or black weights for Dahlia condensed; every display size from 48px to 304px stays at 400.
- Do not set body text in Aeonik 400 or heavier; the page voice is Light 300.
- Do not add drop shadows to cards, buttons or images; separation is colour fields and 1px Plum Hairline #d7cce4 rules.
- Do not round photographs with a border-radius; frame them with an arch mask or a curved section edge instead.
- Do not introduce pink, red or black as an accent; the palette stays in violet, mauve and lavender with one Sky Wash #e6f1ff break.
- Do not loosen the display line-height to 1.2 or more; the word wall depends on 304px/0.68 overlap.
- Do not centre every word-wall line; alternate left and right indents so the emotion words stagger across the width.

## Elevation

There is no shadow anywhere on the page. Depth comes from flat colour fields stacked edge to edge (Lavender Paper, Mauve Panel, Sky Wash, Wisteria Band), from the arch-masked hero photo and the curved Lavender Paper edge that rises over the couple photograph, and from the single 7px-radius footer card sitting inside an Aubergine Frame field, separated by 1px Plum Hairline rules.

## Imagery

Photography is soft, editorial and suggestive rather than explicit: an older woman looking into a hand mirror on a bed of fruit and flowers, a couple seen from behind in white T-shirts against an olive backdrop. Photos are warm, natural-colour and full-bleed or arch-masked, never in rounded cards. The hero background is a crumpled, creased plum paper texture that gives the page its poster quality. Graphic elements are flat and organic: a pale lavender flower blob in the footer card and the icon, a row of multicolour toy silhouettes (teal, pink, sky blue, violet, orange) along the footer, and small sticker-like product cut-outs. Icons are thin line marks in Lilac Haze. Many product cut-outs failed to load during capture, so the real density of stickers is higher than the screenshots show.
