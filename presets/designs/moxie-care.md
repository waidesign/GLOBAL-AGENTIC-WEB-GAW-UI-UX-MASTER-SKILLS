# Moxie Care — Style Reference
> belly skin inside hairline ovals. Frame unretouched body photography in elliptical masks and let a soft Caslon Ionic voice, half in italic, speak in cocoa on cream.

**Theme:** light

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Moxie Care is a cream-canvas maternity and women’s health site that leads with close, unretouched skin: bellies, stretch marks and scars fill full-bleed frames or sit inside overlapping 1px Cocoa #421e10 ellipses. Sections step between Cream #f3edda and Linen #fbfaf2, broken by a Moxie Plum #9a389c brand panel, Marigold #fcce1e, Apricot and Lavender team tiles, and a Butter #f7e399 booking card set on a Cocoa footer. Caslon Ionic does almost all the talking at weight 400 with -0.04em tracking, switching into its italic mid-sentence for the warm phrase, while Fakt Pro carries the plain 24px paragraphs. Calls to action are hairline ovals with italic labels, never filled buttons; there are no shadows anywhere.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Cream #f3edda and Linen #fbfaf2 as alternating section canvases, and Moxie Cocoa #421e10 for running text instead of black.
- Set headlines in Caslon Ionic Regular 400 at 60px/1.1 or 48px/1.1 with -0.04em tracking, and switch to Caslon Ionic Italic for the warm phrase mid-line.
- Keep every call to action a transparent oval: 1px border, 50% radius, 20px 32px padding in the nav and 32px 56px in content, with a Caslon Ionic Italic 18px label.
- Use Fakt Pro 24px/400 at 1.3 line-height for introductory paragraphs, set in two columns.
- Apply 16px radius and 64px 48px padding to Cream testimonial cards with centered Caslon Ionic Italic 20px/1.5 quotes.
- Reserve Marigold #fcce1e for the footer wordmark, footer links and a single hover tile.
- Pair intimate skin photography with outline geometry: hairline ellipses, an outlined wordmark and thin arcs drawn in Cream or Cocoa.

### Don't
- Do not fill buttons with a solid colour; actions are hairline ovals with italic labels.
- Do not use bold weights; every family on the page renders at 400.
- Do not add drop shadows to cards or photos; depth comes from colour steps and outlines.
- Do not set headlines in Fakt Pro; the sans is for paragraphs, inputs and legal text only.
- Do not use pure white #ffffff as a section background; the lightest canvas is Linen #fbfaf2.
- Do not crop people into rectangles with rounded corners alone; use ovals, arches and full-bleed frames.
- Do not let Moxie Plum #9a389c spread beyond the Moxie Birth panel into text or buttons.

## Elevation

There is no measured box-shadow on the page. Depth comes from colour steps (Linen to Cream to Butter to Cocoa), from photographs layered behind 1px Cream or Cocoa hairlines, and from overlapping oval masks. The booking card lifts off the Cocoa footer only by contrast, and black linear fades sit under text on full-bleed photos.

## Imagery

Photography carries the brand. Tight, unretouched close-ups of bellies, stretch marks, scars, tattoos and skin folds fill whole sections in warm, slightly muted natural grading, often with no person’s face. Crops are masked into ellipses, overlapping ovals, arches and circles; the reviews mix colour shots with black-and-white documentary birth photos carrying Linen Instagram handle pills. Team headshots sit on textured neutral backdrops and swap to an oval crop over a Marigold, Apricot or Lavender fill on hover. Linework (an outlined MOXIE wordmark, a thin arc, hairline ellipses) is the only illustration; icons are minimal (Instagram glyph, arrow, plus).

## Layout

A full-bleed, 1600px-wide composition on a 12-column grid with 80px outer and 32px inner gutters. The hero is a 1000px-tall photograph with an overlay nav (hamburger left, oval Book button right), a huge outlined MOXIE wordmark across the middle and a centered 36px serif line near the bottom. The page then alternates centered editorial blocks on Cream and Linen (eyebrow, 60px headline, oval photo strip, two 24px Fakt columns) with edge-to-edge photography: a 50/50 photo and Plum panel split, a full-width photo with stacked centered statements, and a three-column masonry of testimonial cards and arched photos.
