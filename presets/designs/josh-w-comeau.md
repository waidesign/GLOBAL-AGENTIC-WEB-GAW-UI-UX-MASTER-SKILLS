# Josh W. Comeau — Style Reference
> clay mascot on a cloudbank. Float a quiet white reading column beneath layered sky-blue paper clouds, and let blurple ink and raspberry eyebrows carry the play.

**Theme:** light

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Josh W. Comeau is a light, sky-and-paper blog page where a layered cutout cloudscape and a 3D clay avatar of Josh frame an otherwise disciplined white reading column. The hero sky runs Sky Blue #a0d4ee into Cloud Pill #b1dff6 and Cloud Mist #ddeef8 silhouettes, and the same blue returns as a cloud-edged gradient footer, so the page opens and closes in weather. Between them everything is text: Night Ink #0a0c10 Wotfard titles at 22px/600, 16px/400 excerpts on a roomy 1.5 line-height, and spaced uppercase Raspberry Eyebrow #e60067 labels. Comeau Blurple #4242fa is reserved for the wordmark, while soft 8px category chips and one 4px slate Show more button keep the controls friendly rather than salesy.

## Typography

### Wotfard — The single voice of the site, from 12px/400 legal copy through 16px/400 excerpts, 19px/500 sidebar links and 22px/600 article titles. A rounded geometric sans with soft terminals, it stays warm and friendly at every size; every role sits on a 1.5 line-height and almost all at 0 tracking, so hierarchy comes from weight and size, never compression.
- **Substitute:** Figtree, Verdana, sans-serif
- **Licence:** commercial; buy a licence from its foundry, or use the substitute
- **Weights:** 400, 500, 600, 700
- **Sizes:** 12px, 14px, 16px, 17px, 19px, 22px, 24px, 32px
- **Line height:** 1.50
- **Letter spacing:** -1px at 24px (wordmark); +2px at 16px uppercase eyebrows; 0 elsewhere

### Cartograph CF — Inline code inside article titles and subtitles, such as animation-timeline and @starting-style, set heavy at 15.94px and 20.63px on a Cloud Mist #ddeef8 chip. The characterful monospace gives code a crafted, almost hand-lettered feel next to the rounded Wotfard.
- **Substitute:** JetBrains Mono, monospace
- **Licence:** commercial; buy a licence from its foundry, or use the substitute
- **Weights:** 600
- **Sizes:** 15.94px, 20.63px

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Paper #ffffff for the whole reading column and keep Sky Blue #a0d4ee and the Sky Gradient for the hero sky and footer only.
- Set article titles in Wotfard 22px/600 Night Ink #0a0c10 with excerpts at 16px/400 and a 1.5 line-height.
- Set section eyebrows in Raspberry Eyebrow #e60067 Wotfard 16px/500, uppercase, with +2px tracking.
- Reserve Comeau Blurple #4242fa for the Josh Comeau wordmark set at 24px/500 with -1px tracking.
- Build category chips as Cloud Pill #b1dff6 fills with an 8px radius, 5px 12px padding and 14px/400 Night Ink labels.
- Keep the content inside a 1100px max width split into a wide article feed and a narrow sidebar, with 104px section gaps.
- Apply a cutout cloud silhouette edge wherever the sky meets white, both under the header and at the top of the footer.

### Don't
- Do not wrap articles in cards, borders or shadows; each entry is a 22px/600 title, an excerpt and a 16px/600 Read more link on Paper.
- Do not use Comeau Blurple #4242fa as a button fill; the one filled button is Slate Button #262d40 with a 4px radius.
- Do not round buttons into pills; buttons use a 4px radius and chips use 8px.
- Do not tighten body or heading tracking; Wotfard runs at 0 tracking everywhere except the -1px wordmark and +2px eyebrows.
- Do not swap the rounded Wotfard voice for a neutral grotesk such as Inter or Helvetica; use Figtree as the public stand-in.
- Do not use Raspberry Eyebrow #e60067 for links or body text; it marks section labels only.
- Do not replace the clay avatar and paper clouds with stock photography or flat vector people.

## Imagery

There is no photography and no product UI. The imagery is a single illustrated world: layered cutout cloud silhouettes in three pale blues, dotted confetti arcs in blue, violet and pink radiating behind the avatar, and a glossy 3D clay-style figure of Josh in a yellow hoodie sitting cross-legged on a cloud. The footer repeats the trick with the same figure hanging upside down from the cloud edge. Articles carry no thumbnails; personality comes from inline sparkle emoji in excerpts and subtitles. Icons are thin outline glyphs in Night Ink or Harbor Slate, used for header controls, sidebar arrows and footer socials.

## Layout

A centred 1100px container holds everything, with a transparent 48px header floating on the hero sky: wordmark at left, four 16px text links beside it, and four 32px line-icon buttons (search, sound, theme, RSS) at right. The hero is a 536px illustrated band with no headline at all, just layered clouds and the clay avatar sitting off-centre right. Below it the page becomes a two-column blog: a wide left feed of about 620px holding twelve stacked article entries roughly 48px apart, and a narrow right sidebar with Browse by Category chips and a Popular Content list of arrow-led links. The feed fades to white under its last entry and ends in a Show more button. A cloud-edged sky footer with 192px top padding holds the newsletter form at left and three link columns at right. Density is comfortable, driven by generous line-height rather than big gaps.
