# Union Boulangerie — Style Reference
> bakery chalk, cobalt stamp. Combine baking film, thin variable capitals, mono captions and hand-drawn marks across warm paper and bold blue sections.

**Theme:** mixed

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Union Boulangerie is an expressive neighborhood bakery site with a full-screen pastry film, thin uppercase variable typography and a deliberately handmade annotation layer. Cobalt Stamp #2259ec alternates with Flour Paper #f2f0ed, while Crust Ink #55270a keeps reading text warm and Orange Ticket #f25a07 marks selected actions. Serial Blur supplies tightly tracked uppercase headlines, Shinka carries small monospaced descriptions and form labels, and Kiddo interrupts the grid with handwriting. Photographic bakery and pastry collages, irregular drawn outlines and ingredient doodles keep the page lively without card shadows or a uniform pill system.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Cobalt Stamp #2259ec and Flour Paper #f2f0ed as contrasting full-width fields.
- Set large Serial Blur statements at measured weight 80, with 72px/72px and -2.88px tracking for the largest role.
- Pair Shinka 16px/400 reading copy with short Kiddo 16px handwritten annotations.
- Reserve Orange Ticket #f25a07 for contact and small section-label highlights, keeping Crust Ink #55270a on warm paper.
- Keep the measured 30px navigation tabs and 45px form controls compact within the large editorial spaces.
- Apply drawn outlines and ingredient marks around square 0px CSS control boxes instead of standard rounded-card decoration.
- Keep bakery film and photographs at contrasting scales, including offset editorial and location compositions.

### Don't
- Do not normalize Serial Blur weight 80 to a conventional bold marketing headline.
- Do not replace Flour Paper #f2f0ed with stark white throughout the editorial sections.
- Do not use Kiddo for long paragraphs; retain Shinka 16px/400 for reading copy.
- Do not turn every action into an identical capsule; preserve the stamped rectangular and drawn-sketch control types.
- Do not add shadows or a standard nonzero radius system to the flat section fields.
- Do not remove Orange Ticket #f25a07 contact emphasis or spread it across every primary surface.
- Do not reproduce a temporarily blank lazy-media frame as a design choice; follow the loaded bakery imagery captured in the video.
