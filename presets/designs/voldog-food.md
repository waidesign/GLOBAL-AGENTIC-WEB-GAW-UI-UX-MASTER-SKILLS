# VOLDOG Food — Style Reference
> lime ribbon over a butcher’s apron. Frame raw-food commerce in deep pine green, oat and soft gray, then let one acid-lime pill, ribbon or scribble pull the eye at a time.

**Theme:** light

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

VOLDOG Food is a Greek raw-food (B.A.R.F) pet shop that wraps a WooCommerce catalogue in a playful, rounded, sticker-like system. A white canvas alternates with Oat Canvas #f4f1eb bands joined by big curved dividers, while every word is set in deep Butcher Green #204e4a rather than black. Acid Lime Zest #e1e53f carries the conversion pills, the species toggle, a text ribbon that snakes across the page and hand-drawn underlines; Paprika Price #c1452e is kept for prices. Typography is a single round, friendly Peridot PE family, with 38.4px section headings that switch from weight 500 to 700 mid-sentence to stress the key words. Corners are generous (30px cards, 88px hero frame, pill buttons) and depth comes from very faint 34px blurs.

## Typography

### Peridot PE — The only typeface, served by Adobe Fonts as peridot-pe-variable (the file names itself Peridot PE Variable) with full Greek: a round, open geometric grotesque. Headings run at 38.4px, line-height 1.5 to 1.7, mixing weight 500 with 700 in one sentence; nav labels are 15px/600 with +1px tracking; text sits at 14 to 21.6px weight 500. No negative tracking.

- **Licence:** commercial (the font file names it Peridot PE Variable); buy a licence from its foundry, or use the substitute

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Butcher Green #204e4a for every heading, paragraph, label and icon; the site never sets text in black.
- Reserve Lime Zest #e1e53f for the main action on each screen: an 88px-high 9999px pill with 0 37px padding and an 18px/500 Butcher Green label.
- Set section headings in Peridot PE 38.4px at 1.7 line-height and switch the key phrase from weight 500 to 700 inside the same line.
- Apply a 30px radius, Cloud Gray #f2f3f4 fill and 0 40px padding to product cards, with the packshot floating on the gray and no shadow.
- Keep prices in Paprika Price #c1452e at 15px/500, directly under a 20.32px/500 Butcher Green product title.
- Pair each pill button with a round arrow disc at its right end: white on Lime Zest buttons, Lime Zest on white buttons.
- Use Oat Canvas #f4f1eb bands entered through wide curved section edges, and decorate headings with Lime Zest underlines or Carrot Scribble #ec7357 hand-drawn arrows.

### Don't
- Do not use black or a neutral gray for headings; keep Butcher Green #204e4a.
- Do not square off controls; buttons, tabs and toggles are 9999px or 100px pills, and the header SHOP ONLINE button is 50px.
- Do not set whole headings in bold; keep the 38.4px line at weight 500 and bold only the stressed words at 700.
- Do not add negative tracking to Peridot PE; the site runs at 0px except +1px on the 15px/600 nav labels.
- Do not use Lime Zest #e1e53f as text on Paper; it is a fill, ribbon and doodle colour with Butcher Green type on top.
- Do not give product cards drop shadows or borders; use the Cloud Gray #f2f3f4 fill and 30px radius alone.
- Do not use Paprika Price #c1452e for buttons or headings; it marks prices only.

## Imagery

Three kinds of image carry the page. Studio packshots of the frozen meal rolls and kraft treat pouches sit cut-out on the Cloud Gray card fill, so the coloured wrappers do the decorating. Food photography shows a raw meal bowl from above with loose peas and carrot slices scattered around it on Oat Canvas. Lifestyle photography is warm and affectionate: a cut-out collie on a flat olive backdrop in the hero and a woman hugging a dog on the floor at the end, the latter with an arched top crop and a Butcher Fade overlay. Over all of it sit hand-drawn marks: white scribbles in the hero, Lime Zest underlines and a wavy text ribbon, Carrot Scribble arrows and sparks. Icons are thin Butcher Green outlines (search, account, cart, dog and cat faces).
