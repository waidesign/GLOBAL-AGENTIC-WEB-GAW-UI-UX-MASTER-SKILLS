# Poolsuite — Style Reference
> pink system desktop, poolside vhs. Stack beveled cream windows on a flat flamingo field and let dithered leisure footage carry nearly all the colour.

**Theme:** light

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Poolsuite is a leisure radio disguised as a late-eighties operating system: one fixed screen, no page scroll, and a Flamingo Desktop #f6d5d5 wallpaper holding a menu bar, a bottom dock and draggable Window Cream #f9f0e9 applications. Everything is drawn with 1px Ink #000000 outlines, 2–6px corners and hard inset bevels, then softened by one long drop shadow under each window. Pixolde, ChiKareGo2, Everyday and Ishmeria are bitmap faces held at their native 16px and 10px, so the interface reads as pixels rather than type. Colour comes from dithered TV footage, film photography, Pool Cyan #afe2e5 controls and a Legal Pad #faf4c6 calendar. ITC Garamond appears only when a story opens, as a sudden editorial voice inside the machine.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Flamingo Desktop #f6d5d5 as one flat, full-viewport wallpaper and fit the whole product on a single non-scrolling screen.
- Set every application in a Window Cream #f9f0e9 panel with a 1px Ink #000000 border, 6px radius, 6px padding and the 0px 50px 80px -50px shadow at 30% black.
- Keep bitmap faces at their native sizes: Pixolde and ChiKareGo2 at 16px, Everyday at 10px, with -1px tracking on ChiKareGo2 and Everyday.
- Reserve Pool Cyan #afe2e5 for the active or primary key and Flamingo Desktop #f6d5d5 for secondary keys, both with 2px radius and 5px 10px padding.
- Place uppercase Ishmeria 16px window titles at the top right of the title strip and an 18px close control at the top left.
- Build a bottom-centred dock of 80px wide tiles with a colour pixel icon above a 10px Everyday label, and a 32px menu bar pinned to the top corners.
- Let film stills and dithered video supply saturated colour inside Paper #ffffff wells with 4px radius and 1px Ink #000000 outlines.

### Don't
- Do not add a scrolling marketing page; content belongs inside windows, which scroll on their own with a Legal Pad #faf4c6 thumb.
- Do not smooth or scale the bitmap faces to in-between sizes; a 14px Pixolde loses the pixel grid that the look depends on.
- Do not round windows beyond 6px or keys beyond 3px; only the live indicator and small dots use 9999px.
- Do not use soft shadows on buttons; keys take the hard 1px inset bevels and only windows get the long 80px blur.
- Do not use gray body text; Ink #000000 is the only text colour on light surfaces, with hierarchy from face and weight.
- Do not set window chrome in a modern sans such as Inter; ui-sans-serif is only the unstyled browser default behind the bitmap faces.
- Do not spread Progress Teal #44beac through the interface; it belongs to the boot progress bar and nothing else.

## Imagery

Imagery is warm eighties leisure culture: film stills of sports cars, sunbathers, beach parties and pool loungers, plus a looping TV reel of found VHS clips with names like Thomson82.avi and Vegas_Super8.mp4. A fine dot dither over the video gives it the look of a low-resolution CRT. Photos sit in square-cornered or 4px-radius wells with 1px black outlines, never bleeding to the viewport edge. Mixtapes use circular disc artwork that slides out of the player and spins. Icons are small full-colour pixel illustrations in the dock, such as a cocktail, a compact disc, a sunscreen bottle and a brick phone. Mastheads such as the Newsroom sunglasses wordmark and Member Perks title are drawn artwork, not live type. Custom pointer, hand and grab cursors carry the same pixel style.

## Layout

The page is exactly 100vh with no scroll at any measured width: 1600×1000, 768×1024 and 390×844 all report a scroll height equal to the viewport. A 32px menu bar sits in the top corners as two separate tabs, leaving the wallpaper open between them, with the palm square and membership tab on the left and date and clock on the right. The dock is centred on the bottom edge at 720px wide, made of nine 80px tiles. Between them lies free space where windows open at set positions and stay draggable. The player is 607×640 and centred; Newsroom opens at 520×464, Mixtapes at 401×462, Vacation at 663×416, Event Calendar at 328×553 and About at 299×530. Windows overlap freely instead of following a grid. Inside a window, gaps of 4–8px and padding of 5–10px keep density high. At 390px the player narrows to 339×428 and the dock becomes a five-tile strip with arrow keys.
