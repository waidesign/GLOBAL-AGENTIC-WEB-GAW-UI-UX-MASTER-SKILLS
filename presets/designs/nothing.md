# Nothing — Style Reference
> dot-matrix tape on a grey test bench. Set soft serif headlines and pixel-dot labels on a pale grey floor, cut by black video stages and small square-cornered chips.

**Theme:** mixed

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Nothing runs its store like a product lab: a pale Lab Gray #f4f4f4 floor, product cutouts on faint 5% grey trays, and full-bleed black video stages that drop the page into darkness between shopping rows. The voice is split three ways — NType82, a light low-contrast serif at 48px/400 with no tracking, carries every headline; Ndot's dot-matrix capitals stamp eyebrows and the giant footer menu; Geist Mono uppercase runs the buttons and links. Colour is almost absent: Dot Black #040404 and Paper do the work, and a single Signal Yellow #ffc700 sign-up bar is the only hue the interface itself owns. Corners stay small (4px controls, 8px tiles) and nothing casts a shadow.

## Typography

### NType82-Regular — Nothing's own serif and the headline voice: 48px/400 section titles at 1.0 line-height, 32px split-section headings, 24px product names at 1.1 and 18px/400 benefit labels at 1.4. Low-contrast wedge serifs set light and untracked make a hardware shop read like a printed catalogue, not a spec sheet.
- **Substitute:** Newsreader, Georgia, serif
- **Licence:** commercial (the font file names it NType 82); buy a licence from its foundry, or use the substitute
- **Weights:** 400
- **Sizes:** 16px, 18px, 24px, 32px, 48px
- **Line height:** 1.00, 1.10, 1.40
- **Letter spacing:** 0px at every size from 16px to 48px

### Ndot-Regular — The dot-matrix face, uppercase only: 40px/400 footer menu items at 40px line-height, stacked and centred, and the 20px store-locator label. Each letter is built from round dots like a Glyph light panel, so it works as brand signage, never as reading text.

## Components

### Frosted Locator Bar
**Role:** Top-of-page store and region prompt

A 576px-wide Frost White rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8) bar centred 8px below the viewport top inside an 80px header, with 8px radius and no shadow. Centre the label in Ndot-Regular 20px uppercase Dot Black; place a small close cross at the left edge.

### Dot Black Shop Button
**Role:** Primary purchase and discover action

Dot Black #040404 fill, Paper text, 4px radius, 12px 16px padding and a 40px height. Set the label in Geist Mono Variable 12px/400 uppercase at 15px line-height and follow it with a small chevron; no border, no shadow.

### Paper Stage Button
**Role:** Call to action on black video stages

The inverse of the shop button for dark media: Paper #ffffff fill, Dot Black text, 4px radius, 12px 16px padding, 40px high, Geist Mono Variable 12px/400 uppercase label with a chevron. Centre it under the headline or pin it to the lower-right corner of the video.

### Signal Yellow Sign-up Button
**Role:** Newsletter submit

Full 448px column width, Signal Yellow #ffc700 fill, Dot Black Geist Mono Variable 12px uppercase label with a chevron, 4px radius, 12px 16px padding and 40px height. It renders dimmed until the consent checkbox is ticked; no shadow.

### Dot-Matrix Eyebrow
**Role:** Section and campaign label

Ndot-Bold 20px uppercase at 20px line-height and 0px tracking, Dot Black on Lab Gray or Paper on black stages. In split sections it sits alone in the left column; on video stages it sits centred about 24px above the serif headline.

### Serif Section Heading
**Role:** Section and campaign headline

NType82-Regular 48px/400 at 48px line-height with 0px tracking, Dot Black on light and Paper on dark, centred for collection titles or left-aligned at the page edge. Split-section headlines drop to 32px/400 at 32px line-height above a Geist 16px/400 Graphite Copy paragraph.

### Category Tray Card
**Role:** Shop-by-category tile

A square-ish Tray Gray #ececec tile with 8px radius and no border or shadow, holding a centred studio product cutout. Below it, a Graphite Copy Geist Mono Variable 14px/400 uppercase underlined link with a trailing chevron; run four across with 8px gaps.

### Product Feature Card
**Role:** Editorial product pairing

A wide 8px-radius product image on a soft grey studio backdrop, then an NType82-Regular 24px/400 title at 26.4px line-height, a Geist Variable 16px/400 Graphite Copy line at 20.8px, and an underlined Geist Mono Variable 12px uppercase Discover link. Pair two across with 8px gutters.

### Black Video Stage
**Role:** Full-bleed campaign and product film

A 900–1000px full-bleed Dot Black #040404 section playing edge-to-edge video with no radius or frame. Overlay a Paper Ndot-Bold 20px eyebrow, a two-line NType82 48px/400 Paper headline and a Paper Stage Button, centred or anchored bottom-left with 16px insets.

### Benefits Strip
**Role:** Store trust promises

A centred 1152px band on Tray Gray with five equal columns. Each holds a small Graphite Copy pixel-dot line icon above an NType82-Regular 18px/400 label at 25.2px line-height, about 28px apart; no dividers, cards or shadows.

### Newsletter Panel
**Role:** Email sign-up block

A Dot Black #040404 panel inset 8px from the viewport edges with 8px radius, holding a centred NType82 48px Paper heading and a Geist 16px subline. The 448px form has a 48px Field Charcoal input with 4px radius, 8px 16px padding and Geist Mono 12px uppercase placeholder, a consent line with Muted Ash #d8d9d9 underlined links, and the Signal Yellow button.

### Dot-Matrix Footer
**Role:** Site footer and menu

Dot Black #040404 with 112px 32px 32px padding. Stack centred Ndot-Regular 40px/400 uppercase Paper links at 40px line-height and 64px pitch, then 320px-wide Panel Black #111111 rows 48px high with 6px radius, 16px padding and LatteraMonoLL 11px uppercase labels with a right-aligned icon; finish with an 11px LatteraMonoLL link line at both edges.

### Underlined Mono Link
**Role:** Inline product and category link

Geist Mono Variable 12px or 14px/400 uppercase in Graphite Copy #595a5a with a plain underline and a small chevron 8px after the text. Use it under tiles and product cards instead of a button; no background, padding or radius.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Lab Gray #f4f4f4 as the page floor and switch to full-bleed Dot Black #040404 stages for video and the footer.
- Set headlines in NType82-Regular 48px/400 with 48px line-height and 0px tracking; drop to 32px for split-section headings.
- Set eyebrows in Ndot-Bold 20px uppercase at 20px line-height directly above the serif headline.
- Use Dot Black #040404 buttons with 4px radius, 12px 16px padding, a 40px height and Geist Mono Variable 12px/400 uppercase labels plus a small chevron.
- Keep product imagery on Tray Gray #ececec tiles with 8px radius and 8px gaps between tiles.
- Reserve Signal Yellow #ffc700 for the single full-width newsletter sign-up button on the black panel.
- Apply 144px vertical gaps between light sections and keep the footer menu as centred Ndot-Regular 40px uppercase in Paper.

### Don't
- Do not tighten the NType82 headlines or set them bold; they stay 400 with 0px tracking.
- Do not use Ndot for body copy or long sentences; keep it to uppercase labels, eyebrows and the footer menu.
- Do not round buttons into pills; controls use 4px radius and cards 8px.
- Do not add drop shadows to tiles, buttons or panels; use Tray Gray #ececec or Panel Black #111111 tonal steps instead.
- Do not spread Signal Yellow #ffc700 across links, badges or backgrounds; it appears once.
- Do not introduce brand reds or blues from campaign imagery into the interface; UI colour stays Dot Black, Paper and greys.
- Do not set button labels in the serif; actions are Geist Mono Variable 12px uppercase.

## Elevation

There are no shadows anywhere. Depth is tonal and translucent: category tiles are a 5% Graphite Copy wash (Tray Gray #ececec) over Lab Gray, footer rows are a 5% white lift (Panel Black #111111) over Dot Black, the newsletter input is a 60% charcoal wash, and the store-locator bar is 80% Frost White floating over the hero. Separation otherwise comes from hard switches between light floor and black stage.

## Imagery

Imagery is product-first and studio-lit: earbuds, headphones, phones, watches and speakers shot as clean cutouts on soft grey gradients inside 8px-radius tiles, with the products' own transparent shells, red dots and coloured bodies (pink, yellow, teal, orange) supplying all the colour the interface withholds. Campaign moments switch to cinematic dark video — a headphone rotating in blackness, a celebrity on set, a figure in a white-lit room — played full-bleed without frames. The hero is a tactile collage of parcel tape printed with giant dot-matrix letters, fragile stickers and postage stamps. Iconography is tiny pixel-dot line icons in Graphite Copy, echoing the Ndot type.

## Layout

The page is a full-bleed vertical stack on Lab Gray with almost no visible grid chrome. A 1000px hero image fills the first screen, with only a translucent 576px store-locator bar centred at the top instead of a conventional nav. Light sections use a split composition: a dot-matrix eyebrow on the left third and a serif headline, Geist paragraph and black button starting at the page midpoint. Product rows run edge to edge with 8px gutters — a four-up category strip, an asymmetric staggered trio of tiles under 'Going pink?', a two-up speaker pair — separated by roughly 144px of empty floor. Between them, 900–1000px black video stages hold either a centred eyebrow, two-line headline and white button, or a bottom-left headline with a corner button. A centred 1152px five-column benefits band, an 8px-inset black newsletter panel with a 448px form column, and a centred Ndot footer menu over 320px utility rows close the page.
