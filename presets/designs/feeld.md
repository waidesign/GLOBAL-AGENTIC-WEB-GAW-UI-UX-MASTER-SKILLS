# Feeld — Style Reference
> velvet midnight, neon confession. Build a near-black stage where thin rounded headlines, magenta glyph icons and white pills float over intimate film footage.

**Theme:** dark

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Feeld is a near-black, after-dark dating site set on a Midnight Canvas #090516 that never lightens, with intimate full-bleed film footage and phone mock-ups carrying all the warmth. Headlines are huge and whisper-thin: FEELD Edge at 111px and 56px in its Regular Rounded cut, tracked slightly tight, with small magenta #e72abd line icons dropped between words. Colour arrives in saturated blocks rather than washes: plum, cobalt, clover and oxblood testimonial cards with one square corner, a periwinkle #b1baff pill, an acid-lime #e5ff4a stat. ABC Diatype keeps body and interface copy plain and quiet, and every action is a 9999px pill with no shadow.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Midnight Canvas #090516 for every section and the footer; let footage, cards and the Butter Banner #fffbcc supply all contrast.
- Set headlines in FEELD Edge at its thin Regular Rounded weight: 111px with -1px tracking for the hero, 56px/1.0 with -0.56px for section titles.
- Place small Feeld Magenta #e72abd outline icons inline between headline words, sized to the x-height, as visual punctuation.
- Use 9999px pills for every action: Paper #ffffff with Onyx #131313 16px/400 labels and 16px 28px padding by default.
- Reserve Periwinkle Pill #b1baff for secondary pills and Feeld Magenta #e72abd for the single closing sign-up pill.
- Give testimonial cards a 12px 12px 0px 12px radius so one corner stays square, like a chat bubble, with 16px padding.
- Keep body copy in ABC Diatype at 16px to 18px with 1.5 line-height in Bone #ededed or Mist #c7c8d1.

### Don't
- Do not add drop shadows to cards, pills or the navigation; separate layers with tone and 1px Dusk Hairline #2b263a borders.
- Do not set headlines in a bold weight; FEELD Edge stays at its thin Regular Rounded cut, with SemiBold only for the rotating hero word.
- Do not use pure white #ffffff for long text; body and headings use Bone #ededed.
- Do not spread Feeld Magenta #e72abd across backgrounds or large fills; it is for icons, the final CTA and the wordmark.
- Do not use square or 8px-radius buttons; every button and tag is a pill (9999px or 200px).
- Do not mix the four testimonial card colours inside one card; each card takes one flat fill.
- Do not switch sections to a light background; the only light block is the Butter Banner #fffbcc magazine promo.

## Elevation

The page has no box-shadows at all. Depth comes from tone and light: Aubergine Chip #181424 pills one step above Midnight Canvas, 1px Dusk Hairline #2b263a borders, a translucent black glass capsule behind the navigation, saturated card fills, and footage that glows against the dark. Overlap is handled by edge masks that fade marquees into the canvas, not by cast shadows.

## Imagery

Imagery is intimate lifestyle film and photography: warm, low-light, close-cropped footage of people embracing or lying together, natural skin tones and grain, darkened so white type reads over it. Profile and event photography is casual and diverse, styled as real app users with names, ages and orientations overlaid on a dark bottom fade. Product moments are phone mock-ups and cropped app screens on saturated plum, cobalt and green panels, with chat bubbles and tag pills spilling out. Media corners are 12px to 16px, often with one corner left square. Iconography is thin magenta line glyphs set inline in headlines, line-icon tab and social marks in Bone, and circular avatar stacks. Article thumbnails show suggestive but non-explicit editorial photography.

## Layout

A single dark column runs edge to edge on Midnight Canvas, with content held inside a 1440px container (58px side padding, so text starts about 138px in at 1600px). The hero is a full-bleed 1000px video under an 88px transparent header; its headline is broken across three staggered lines that step from left to centre to right, with an avatar badge at bottom left and the white CTA pill at bottom right. Below, sections alternate between centred statements (thin two-line headlines with inline icons over a phone mock-up and two marquee rows of tag pills, a full-width profile-card carousel) and asymmetric splits (a headline and pill at left with two feature cards at right; a media panel at left with a headline, paragraph and wide pill at right). Section gaps run around 96px to 144px, testimonial and article rows scroll horizontally past the right edge with small dot pagination, and the page closes on a centred 122px Join Feeld, a five-column footer and a giant magenta wordmark.
