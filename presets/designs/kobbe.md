# Kobbe — Style Reference
> harbor sketch, quiet ledger. Pair warm neutral software panels with an illustrated waterfront, offset editorial headings, and compact dark controls.

**Theme:** light

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Kobbe is a warm, low-contrast analytics site where small editorial annotations sit to the left of larger feature statements. Chalk Paper #faf9f8 carries Charcoal Ink #2b2b2b, with Warm Mist #f5f3f0 panels and Stone Rule #e4e2dd separators. Switzer stays mostly at weight 500, reaching 48px with -1.2px tracking in the hero; the supporting copy is softer Pebble Gray #75736e. Product screenshots sit in gently lifted frames, while hand-drawn people along a quay and a watercolor village give the otherwise precise software presentation its human character.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Chalk Paper #faf9f8 as the continuous warm canvas and Charcoal Ink #2b2b2b for headings and primary actions.
- Set the hero in Switzer 48px/500 with 60px leading and -1.2px tracking; set section headings at 36px/500 with 45px leading.
- Keep the measured 1280px outer content limit and 1024px product-image span, with 32px horizontal insets.
- Apply 10px corners to main controls and panels, keeping compact navigation controls at 8px.
- Reserve the deep layered shadow for product screenshots; keep Warm Mist #f5f3f0 feature and pricing surfaces quiet.
- Pair 14px annotations in the left column with larger feature statements to the right, repeating 96px section padding.
- Keep illustrated people, animals and the waterfront separate from the precise product UI; use original artwork appropriate to the new brand.

### Don't
- Do not replace Chalk Paper #faf9f8 with pure white or a cool blue-gray canvas.
- Do not turn Charcoal Ink #2b2b2b actions into colorful gradient or full-pill controls; keep the main 10px radius.
- Do not use bold display weights by default; the measured hero and section heads use Switzer 500.
- Do not list Geist Mono as an active page typeface; only Switzer was observed rendering in this capture.
- Do not center every feature introduction; preserve the offset annotation and right-hand statement composition.
- Do not apply the product-image shadow to every tile; use Warm Mist #f5f3f0 and Stone Rule #e4e2dd for quieter structure.
- Do not replace the irregular illustration language with generic glossy 3D objects; preserve the editorial hand-drawn role using original artwork.
