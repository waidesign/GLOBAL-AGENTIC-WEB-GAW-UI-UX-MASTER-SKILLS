# Don't Board Me — Style Reference
> red leash on bubblegum paper. Shout in giant condensed red capitals over pastel pink fields, then let hand-drawn dogs, tennis balls and pill stickers do the talking.

**Theme:** light

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Don't Board Me is a pet-sitting site styled like a children's picture book shouted through a megaphone. A cream hero hands over to long bubblegum-pink #f4ced3 fields stacked with huge pale circles, then cools into a sky-blue #afd8fb contact and pricing run and ends on a bone-white footer. Almost every word is Leash Red #e33529: Bayon capitals at 206–326px with a 0.78 line-height and roughly -2% tracking jam whole lines edge to edge, while tiny uppercase Neue Montreal 500 labels sit beside them. Pool Blue #2b6786 takes over on the blue sections, tennis-ball yellow #fff500 bounces across as decoration, and controls are fat rotated pills with a trailing dot.

## Typography

### Bayon — The voice of the site: a tall, narrow, single-weight display face set entirely in uppercase. It runs from 33.33px pill labels to 325.56px section titles at a 0.78 line-height and about -2% tracking (-6.51px at 325.56px, -2.13px at 106.67px), so lines stack tight and fill the screen width like a poster.

- **Weights:** 400
- **Sizes:** 17.78px, 33.33px, 46.67px, 77.78px, 80px, 106.67px, 206.67px, 262.22px, 325.56px
- **Line height:** 0.78, 1.00

- **Licence:** commercial; buy a licence from its foundry, or use the substitute

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Leash Red #e33529 for nearly all type on pink and cream sections: headlines, nav links, labels and footer links.
- Set display headlines in Bayon uppercase at 206.67px/400 with a 0.78 line-height and -4.13px tracking, and let the lines nearly touch.
- Keep small text in Neue Montreal 15.56px/500 uppercase at a 1.0 line-height with no tracking; never set body copy in lowercase.
- Apply Bubblegum Canvas #f4ced3 as the main background and layer Petal Circle #f3c3cb discs and arcs behind content for depth.
- Use 166.67px pill buttons in Leash Red or Pool Blue with a Bayon 33.33px label and a trailing dot, tilted a few degrees when they float over illustration.
- Reserve Pool Blue #2b6786 for type and controls on the Sky Wash #afd8fb sections, swapping it in for red.
- Pair big headlines with hand-drawn dog illustrations and scattered Tennis Ball #fff500 balls instead of icons or photography.

### Don't
- Do not use bold or mixed-weight sans headlines; display type is Bayon 400 uppercase with a 0.78 line-height, never Neue Montreal.
- Do not introduce dark or black sections; the darkest fill is Pool Blue #2b6786 and near-black Brush Ink #1d1d1d stays inside illustrations.
- Do not add drop shadows to buttons or pricing cards; the only shadow is the soft pink rgba(221, 198, 202, 0.4) -3px 4px 14px on process cards.
- Do not set Tennis Ball #fff500 as text or a button fill; it is only for ball illustrations and small markers.
- Do not use square or 4px buttons; actions are 166.67px pills or 9999px discs.
- Do not frame content in a narrow centred column; headlines run edge to edge across the 1600px viewport.
- Do not use stock photography above the fold; photos appear only in the small rounded Instagram wall near the footer.

## Imagery

Illustration does almost all the work. The hero is a giant flat-white sheepdog drawn with dry black brush strokes and holding a red leash; services show soft grainy dogs inside pale circles, and the process cards use flat pastel figures (blue, lilac, pink) on lilac panels. Tennis balls are the recurring motif: as the preloader you have to bounce, as huge Biscuit Card clouds with white seams, and as small Tennis Ball yellow sprites scattered over the blue contact section. Line icons are thin red single-stroke drawings in round discs. Real photography only appears in the Instagram wall near the footer: warm natural photos of people with dogs, cropped square or landscape with lightly rounded corners and scattered loosely like snapshots.

## Layout

The page is full-bleed with no max-width container: content sits 36px from the viewport edges and headlines are sized to span almost the whole width. A transparent 69px header holds the dachshund mark and stacked wordmark at left, five centred uppercase links, and a red Book Now tab hanging from the top-right edge. The hero splits a two-line red headline between the top-left and the lower-right, with a huge white dog illustration in the middle. Below, the page is one long scroll of colour fields instead of separate sections: a cream-to-pink transition behind a cloud of white tennis balls, a 325px OUR SERVICES title, a pinned service carousel inside a giant pink circle, a pinned how-it-works stage where four tilted cards stack one by one, a testimonial timeline, then Sky Wash contact and four-column pricing, an Instagram photo wall and a two-column link footer. Sections are separated by colour changes and circles, not rules or padding.
