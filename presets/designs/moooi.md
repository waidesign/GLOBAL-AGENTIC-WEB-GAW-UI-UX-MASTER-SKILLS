# Moooi — Style Reference
> midnight showroom, cream plinth. Stage full-bleed interiors and craft films behind large flared-sans headlines, then drop into a white catalogue of objects on cream.

**Theme:** mixed

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Moooi is a Dutch design house whose storefront behaves like a darkened gallery with a very quiet shop attached. The home page stacks 1000px full-bleed scenes, each carrying a centred SangBleu Sans 76.8px/400 headline in white, broken once by a Paper category rail and once by a Tokyo Blue #2b3e7b split panel. Past the menu, the catalogue flips to Paper #ffffff, with objects cut out on Plinth Cream #f7f4ef and tiny tracked Gill Sans MT captions. Showroom Graphite #1f1f1f carries nearly all text and every primary button; Linen Sand #e3d9c6 marks only the active filter. Depth comes from image and scrim, with soft shadows reserved for pills and the floating buy card.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use full-bleed 1000px scenes on the home page, each with a centred SangBleu Sans 76.8px/400/92.16px headline in Paper #ffffff at -1.54px tracking.
- Set uppercase eyebrows above headlines in Gill Sans MT 14.93px/400 with 1.49px tracking, never in the display face.
- Place every catalogue object on Plinth Cream #f7f4ef with 8px padding and square corners, on a Paper #ffffff page.
- Keep product captions tiny and centred: Gill Sans MT 11.73px/700 uppercase title, 11.73px/500 designer and 10.67px/500 price with 1px tracking.
- Reserve Showroom Graphite #1f1f1f fill and white 16px/500 type for the primary action, on a 6px radius at 48–51px high.
- Apply a 45% black backdrop behind 533px Paper drawers that slide in from the left for Menu and the right for Filter & Sort.
- Pair a heart button with its action in one joined control: 6px outer corners on the left piece, 0px 6px 6px 0px on Contact.

### Don't
- Do not set catalogue pages on a dark field; the black belongs to scenes and the footer, while product browsing stays on Paper #ffffff.
- Do not round product media or scene images; keep images at 0px and save 42.67px for pills and 19px for the floating buy card.
- Do not use heavy 600–700 weights for SangBleu Sans headlines; keep 400 at 76.8px and go lighter, 300, for product names.
- Do not spread Tokyo Blue #2b3e7b across interface controls; it is a single editorial panel, and buttons stay Showroom Graphite #1f1f1f.
- Do not use Tiempos Fine for product titles; keep its 102.4px/300 italic for editorial index pages such as Stories.
- Do not make the eyebrow or card caption sentence case; Gill Sans MT in those roles is always uppercase with 0.6–1.49px positive tracking.
- Do not put drop shadows on catalogue cards; keep them flat on Plinth Cream #f7f4ef and reserve the soft offset shadow for the floating buy card and film pills.

## Imagery

Imagery does the storytelling. Full-bleed films and stills show dusk architectural interiors, a slowly unfolding Peaks sofa, a ceramic Egg Vase holding wild flowers against patterned wall tile, and an embroidered Extinct Animals tapestry stitching itself into being across the 25 Years scene. Catalogue pictures are the opposite: objects clipped cleanly and centred on Plinth Cream with no props, and hovering a card crossfades into a dramatic room setting such as a black chandelier above a red sofa. Colour stays moody and saturated in scenes, pale in the shop. Icons are thin line glyphs for heart, bag, search and account in Showroom Graphite or white. Images are never rounded.

## Layout

The home page is a vertical stack of 1000px full-bleed scenes under a transparent header, opened by a 40px black promo strip. Copy is centred over each image; a 529px Paper rail with five tiles interrupts after the first scene, and a 1200px split panel pairs an image with a Tokyo Blue text column. Inner pages change register completely: a centred breadcrumb, 48px title and 704px Tiempos paragraph sit above a mixed-width grid whose first card spans 784px beside narrower cards, switchable to six small columns. Product pages place a large cut-out object left and a 371px buy column right, then a 742px lead paragraph with spec rows, full-bleed room scenes, and a floating buy card at bottom right. Sizes scale with viewport; at 390px the grid drops to two columns.
