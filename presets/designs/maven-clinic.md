# Maven Clinic — Style Reference
> evergreen nursery, mint heartbeat. Frame warm family photography inside deep forest-green panels, switch on mint for every action, and let one serif italic word carry the feeling.

**Theme:** mixed

Source measurements are normalized; roles and recommendations are interpreted. Font summary lists are independent, not paired by position. HTML examples are reconstructions, not source components.

Maven Clinic is a deep-forest healthcare site that alternates Forest Canopy #013126 panels with warm Linen #ede9e3 inserts, each rounded and inset like a page inside a page. Candid, golden-lit family photography carries the emotion, while Mint Signal #58eda2 is the single switched-on colour for actions, eyebrows and markers. Helvetica Now Display runs light at 300 for every large headline, and a single Ivar Display italic word drops into each one (healthcare, all in one place, improving care) so clinical claims read as personal. Small 4px buttons, 8px cards and soft teal-to-linen gradient tiles keep the system calm, precise and unshowy.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use Forest Canopy #013126 for the hero frame, dark sections and the footer panel, and Linen #ede9e3 for light inset panels.
- Set large headlines in Helvetica Now Display 300 at 76px, 62px or 56px with 1.0 line-height and 0 tracking.
- Pair every major headline with one Ivar Display italic 400 word or phrase at a larger size than the sans (91px against 76px).
- Reserve Mint Signal #58eda2 for primary fills, outline borders, badge text and marker dots on dark sections.
- Keep buttons at a 4px radius with 18px/500 labels and -0.36px tracking; switch to Jade Fill #028c74 on light sections.
- Keep light sections inset into the dark page with 12px or 24px corners so each band reads as a sheet laid on Forest Canopy.
- Set testimonial quotes at 24px/400 with 1.3 line-height and -0.48px tracking in Quote Charcoal #222222 on Mist White #f5fff9.

### Don't
- Do not set display headlines bold; Maven uses Helvetica Now Display 300 at 56–76px.
- Do not use pure white #ffffff as a page canvas; light sections are Linen #ede9e3 or Oat Paper #e7e0d7.
- Do not round buttons into pills; the action radius is 4px and 9999px is only for marker dots.
- Do not set whole headlines or body copy in Ivar Display; it appears only as italic accent words.
- Do not add drop shadows to cards or photos; separate them with tonal steps and 8px corners.
- Do not use Mint Signal #58eda2 as a fill on Linen sections; use Jade Fill #028c74 there.
- Do not use cold stock photography; images are warm, candid, low-light family moments.

## Imagery

Photography is the emotional core: candid, warm, often low-light family and patient moments (parents with children, midlife couples, a pregnant woman in sunlight), graded golden and slightly desaturated so skin tones glow against the green. Images are cropped tight to faces and hands, sit in 8px-radius cards or fill whole sections edge to edge, and carry a dark corner fade where white text sits on them. Product imagery is light and soft: stacked specialty chips, a dashed wireframe globe with country tags and chat-bubble icons sit inside gradient bento tiles. Decorative graphics are thin coloured curves ending in small dots, and outcome stats use thick gradient ring arcs on Ring Sand tracks. Customer logos appear in white on the dark marquee.

## Layout

The page is a stack of rounded sheets on a deep Forest Canopy field under a slim Linen announcement bar and a floating white 4px-radius nav card that slides away on scroll-down. The hero is a full-viewport photograph framed by 12px of Forest Canopy, with the headline, subtitle and two buttons in the lower-left third over thin line-and-dot curves. A centered 62px statement leads into four tall life-stage photo cards and three NEW service cards. A Linen panel inset with 12px corners holds a left headline, a right lead and a three-column bento grid of 8px tiles. A full-bleed photo gives way to a Linen outcomes band with 24px top corners and four ring stats split by hairlines. A split section pairs a headline and audience links with a tall photo; a logo marquee, a jade member-stories panel, an Oat Paper closing block under a giant soft circle and a Forest footer panel close the page. Sections sit 48px from the viewport edges with roughly 96px vertical rhythm.
