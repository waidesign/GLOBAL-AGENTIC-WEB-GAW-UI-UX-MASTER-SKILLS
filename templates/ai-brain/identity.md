# Project Identity & Visual Direction

## Design Direction
- **Voice**: Clean, professional, distinctive, production-grade.
- **Anti-Slop Discipline**: Reject generic AI aesthetics, random gradients, meaningless card wrappers, and unrequested animations.
- **Visual Pacing**: Deliberate negative space, consistent rhythm, clear visual hierarchy.

## Typography
- Single or paired type families with high contrast.
- Strict type scale adhering to standard proportional ratios.
- Headings use `text-balance` / tight tracking; body copy uses `text-pretty` with line lengths under 80 characters.
- Tabular figures (`tabular-nums`) for numbers and data tables.

## Color & Surface
- Purpose-built palette rooted in the product domain.
- Semantic tokens: primary, secondary, muted, accent, destructive, background, surface.
- High accessibility contrast ratio (minimum WCAG AA 4.5:1).
